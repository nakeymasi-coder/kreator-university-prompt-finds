Happy Tuesday, Kreators! 💙 I’ve been exploring how jeweled lettering looks on everyday objects—a notebook, a windshield sunshade, and luggage. These are AI design concepts, and the little details make a big difference! 👀✨
A useful mockup prompt needs three things:
1. Name the object and exactly where the artwork belongs.
2. Tell it to follow the surface, perspective, seams, and lighting.
3. Keep the wording readable and away from folds and edges.
📋 TRY THIS with your own artwork:
“Using my uploaded artwork as the reference, create one realistic [OBJECT] mockup. Place the design on [EXACT SURFACE]. Preserve the wording and colors. Match the object’s perspective, material, seams, and lighting. Keep important details away from folds and edges. Show the entire object in a clean lifestyle setting. No extra text or watermark.”
Check every letter and edge afterward. A pretty mockup still needs a second look! 😂
